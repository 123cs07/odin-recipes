This page will give you recipie of Lemon Water.

    Ingredients you need.
  1. 1 glass of water.
  2. 1 yellow lemon medium size.
  3. 1 spoon of sugar cube.
  4. 1 spoon of lemon water spieces.
    Steps to prepare lemon water.
  • Sequeez lemon in glass of water.
  • Add sugar and mix it in water.
  • Add all spies and shake it till all mixes well.
  • Put it in freezer or add ice cube and serve it.
