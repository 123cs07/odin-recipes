This page will give you recipie of Spicy fried rice.

    Ingredients you need

  1. 200gm of Banaspati Rice.
  2. 250ml of water.
  3. 1 bowl of chopped tomato, onion.
  4. 4 green chilly, chopped ginger, garlic.
  5. 2 bowl of fresh cut vegitables.
  6. Spices for flavour.

    Steps to cook fried rice.

  • Wash rice with clean water and soaked for 15 minutes.
  • Take pan and 2 spoon of cooking oil and heat it.
  • Add 2 spoon of jeera and stir it then add copped onion.
  • When onion become golden color add tomato, chilly, ginger, garlic cook it for 5 minutes.
  • Now add rice, water, vegetablas, speices and cover the pan and let rice cook for 15 to 20 minutes.
  • Check rice in between if cooked or not, then serve it with green mint, yogurt etc.
