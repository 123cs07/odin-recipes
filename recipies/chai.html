This page will give your recipie of CHAI.

Ingredients: Water, Ginger, Milk, Sugar, Stove.

    How to make Chai?

  • Turn on the stove.
  • Add 1 cup water in a pan boil it.
  • Add 1 spoon of tea, 1 clove of ginger and boil it for 2 mintutes.
  • Now add milk, 1 spoon of sugar and mix it and boil for 5 minutes.
  • Turn off the stove and filter it a cup.
